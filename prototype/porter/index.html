Porter capstone · Step 4 prototype

AI vehicle-sizing assistant

First-time Porter customers arrive at vehicle selection with a physical job — a sofa, a fridge, a room of boxes — and no way to turn it into a vehicle category. This adds two things to the existing flow: a way to describe the job and get a sized recommendation back, with the reasoning and comparable moves shown alongside it — plus one check before dispatch on the bookings the system has already flagged as uncertain.

Reading this prototype

Screens marked New are the proposed addition. Screens marked Existing are Porter's current flow, included so the join between the two is visible. Matching is deterministic rules over the typed text, not a live model — the reasoning line and recommendation change with what you enter. Fares and reviews are illustrative.
